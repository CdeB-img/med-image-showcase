## 4. SEM recycling: SEM-AUDIT

### Responsibility

`SEM-AUDIT-D` receives conversation, previous state, candidate state, confirmed decisions, optional explicitly supplied Knowledge and raw-output evidence. It returns only `findings[]`. It does not emit a replacement state, mutate its input, auto-fix science, choose a Project decision or write to product state.

The finding contract defines the 19 required classes, including omission, relation, polarity/causality, ownership/promotion, conceptual-plane, history, provenance, ambiguity, contradiction and raw-output failures. Every emitted finding has a content-derived stable ID, source evidence, candidate pointer, expected boundary, rationale, owner, `autoFixAllowed=false` and an open status.

### Deterministic guards

G1–G12 are implemented or directly formalized: candidate versus primary adoption; local practice versus Project decision; association versus causality; partial versus systematic availability; real semantic change for correction; no reflexive relation by default; separation of Method/MeasurementDefinition/Measure/Observable/Phenomenon/BiomarkerRole/EndpointRole; raw-output persistence; negative polarity preservation; inactive rejected/superseded history; answered clarification handling; and no UNKNOWN-to-confirmed transition without new evidence.

`SEM-AUDIT-L` is contract-only, non-mutating, not product-integrated and not qualified. No provider call was made.

### Historical fixture evidence

The existing I01–I08 experimental corpus remains immutable. Five directly relevant families, I04–I08, are represented by **8 minimal visible fixture variants** linked to their original ablation artifacts:

- I04: partial/systematic availability and missing raw evidence;
- I05: corrected primary response, exploratory recurrence and baseline/delta separation;
- I06: distinct methods, unknown principal measure and secondary population;
- I07: association preserved versus prohibited causal promotion;
- I08: candidate preserved versus primary-endpoint promotion.

I01–I03 remain historical experimental evidence but were not transformed into artificial fixtures because no mission guard required a case-specific projection. No scenario identifier or domain-specific term exists in the audit core.

The required failures are detected, the safe I07/I08 variants remain free of the prohibited false positives, and raw-output absence produces `RAW_OUTPUT_NOT_PERSISTED` rather than a fabricated scientific-understanding failure.

## 7. QRY and Adaptive Research Workspace

The QRY boundary is formalized but not implemented. It consumes current/adopted state, unknowns, ambiguities, contradictions, missing information, open decisions, dependencies, Knowledge requests, question/answer history, user role and workflow stage. It can return `ASK`, `FINISH`, `FINISH_WITH_OPEN_DECISIONS`, `REQUEST_KNOWLEDGE`, `REQUEST_SPECIALIST` or `STOP` with information-gain and branch impact.

QRY does not reconstruct conversation, own truth or necessarily formulate final wording. An answered question is not repeated without a documented reopening condition. The Adaptive Research Workspace remains a projection of the living Research Project.
