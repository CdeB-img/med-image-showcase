## Roadmap

### TERRITORY-WAVE-FOUNDATIONS

Établir les concepts transverses dont dépendent les comparaisons et les mesures de plusieurs spécialités.

Territoires : modalities-acquisition, measurements-biomarkers, physics-instrumentation, quality-safety, informatics-workflows.

Règle : Select HIGH priority nodes inside these territories only when the catalogue records an actionable source, assertion, provenance or synthesis gap.

### TERRITORY-WAVE-CLINICAL-CORE

Couvrir les grandes spécialités anatomiques et les questions cliniques transverses à forte valeur documentaire.

Territoires : anatomy-specialties, clinical-applications.

Règle : The catalogue resolves actual order from coverage, dependencies and priority; the territory supplies no hand-picked node.

### TERRITORY-WAVE-QUANTITATIVE

Étendre les biomarqueurs, mesures et comparaisons quantitatives au-delà des pilotes existants.

Territoires : measurements-biomarkers, research-evidence.

Règle : Measurement campaigns require explicit method, unit, applicability and provenance gaps in the catalogue.

### TERRITORY-WAVE-COMPUTATIONAL

Couvrir traitement d'image, radiomique et IA selon des contrats de validation indépendants des applications cliniques.

Territoires : computational-imaging, quality-safety, informatics-workflows.

Règle : No internal dataset, training job or deployed model may be created by a scientific enrichment campaign.

### TERRITORY-WAVE-INTERVENTIONAL

Documenter guidage, techniques et résultats d'intervention sans créer de workflow exécutable ni de recommandation clinique automatique.

Territoires : interventional-imaging, quality-safety, clinical-applications.

Règle : Only documentary scientific objects are eligible; operational procedure configuration remains out of scope.

### TERRITORY-WAVE-FUTURE

Réévaluer périodiquement les technologies émergentes et les zones adjacentes conditionnelles.

Territoires : modalities-acquisition, physics-instrumentation, computational-imaging, research-evidence.

Règle : Future nodes require an explicit territory revision before the catalogue can schedule them.

## Limites

- The model is exhaustive at territory, domain, subdomain and knowledge-area granularity, not an enumeration of every possible atomic scientific concept.
- Current coverage is derived from exact catalog identities and concept-family compatibility; semantic reassignment is reported but never applied.
- Potential KnowledgeNode and page totals are planning ranges, not forecasts of scientific evidence or search demand.
- No territory node is scientific evidence and no projection is publication authorization.
