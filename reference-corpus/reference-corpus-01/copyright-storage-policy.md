# Copyright, licence and storage decision — REFERENCE-CORPUS-01

This is a conservative corpus-acquisition decision, not legal advice and not a generic repository policy.

## Locally retained

Five FDA guidance PDFs are retained unchanged. The FDA website policy states that FDA website content is public domain unless otherwise noted and may be republished or reused. Each record preserves the canonical FDA URL, retrieval date and SHA-256. FDA names/logos remain subject to the separate trademark boundary; corpus inclusion does not imply endorsement.

## Remote-only

- ICH, EMA, European Commission/EUR-Lex, CNIL, EDPB, WHO, CDISC, SCDM and QIBA/RSNA: licence or file-level redistribution terms were not sufficiently established in this bounded pass.
- NIH/NCI operational templates: U.S. federal provenance is recorded, but linked-file authorship and file-specific terms were not exhaustively verified.
- ClinicalTrials.gov uploaded documents: sponsor or investigator copyright may subsist; public availability is not treated as permission to redistribute.
- ANR, DGOS/Ministry and France 2030 documents: official URLs are recorded, but local-copy and redistribution terms remain `UNKNOWN` until a source-specific reuse review.

For every `UNKNOWN`, the default action is metadata plus official URL only. No licence is inferred from accessibility, institutional status or absence of a visible copyright notice.

## Future acquisition gate

A remote-only source may gain a local copy only after recording an explicit licence/reuse basis, an original official URL, retrieval date, unchanged file digest, version/currentness and any source-specific attribution conditions. Restricted material must never be published through this repository.
