# Copyright, licence and storage decision — REFERENCE-CORPUS-01

This is a conservative corpus-acquisition decision, not legal advice and not a generic repository policy.

## Locally retained

Six FDA guidance PDFs are retained unchanged. The FDA website policy states that FDA website content is public domain unless otherwise noted and may be republished or reused. Each record preserves the canonical FDA URL, retrieval date and SHA-256. FDA names/logos remain subject to the separate trademark boundary; corpus inclusion does not imply endorsement.

One European Commission-hosted CTR Q&A PDF is retained unchanged. The Commission legal notice applies CC BY 4.0 to Commission-owned site content unless otherwise indicated. The registry preserves attribution, official locator, version, publication date and SHA-256; the document's non-binding status remains explicit.

## Remote-only

- ICH, EMA/EUR-Lex, CNIL, EDPB, WHO, CDISC, SCDM and QIBA/RSNA: licence or file-level redistribution terms were not sufficiently established in this bounded pass.
- MDCG: official EC hosting and current catalogue status were verified, but the files state that they are not European Commission documents. The Commission-owned-content licence was therefore not extended to them without explicit ownership evidence.
- ANSM, ECRIN, ANRS and NCI/IROC operational documents: official provenance is recorded, but file-level local-storage and redistribution rights were not sufficiently established.
- NIH/NCI operational templates: U.S. federal provenance is recorded, but linked-file authorship and file-specific terms were not exhaustively verified.
- ClinicalTrials.gov and platform-trial uploaded documents: sponsor or investigator copyright may subsist; public availability is not treated as permission to redistribute. Explicit `NO` restrictions for REMAP-CAP UK, PRINCIPLE and PRACTICAL are preserved rather than generalized.
- STEP, REMAP-CAP, PRINCIPLE, PRACTICAL and I-SPY content reviewed by `DOCUMENTARY-EVIDENCE-CLOSURE-01`: content-level access is recorded separately from storage rights. REMAP-CAP UK, PRINCIPLE and PRACTICAL restrictions remain explicit `NO`; other platform file rights remain `UNKNOWN`. No platform binary was committed.
- BioLINCC SPRINT and ORCHID study documents: NHLBI's general website policy was identified, but file-level authorship and third-party rights were not exhaustively established for the investigator-authored trial documents. All eight files therefore remain `CONTENT_ACCESSIBLE_NOT_STORED`, with local storage, repository commit and redistribution classified `UNKNOWN` and no binary committed.
- ANR, DGOS/Ministry and France 2030 documents: official URLs are recorded, but local-copy and redistribution terms remain `UNKNOWN` until a source-specific reuse review.

For every `UNKNOWN`, the default action is metadata plus official URL only. No licence is inferred from accessibility, institutional status or absence of a visible copyright notice.

`DERIVED_PATTERN_ANALYSIS_ALLOWED = YES` in the closure projection authorizes only later non-reproductive structural analysis of facts, relationships and documentary placement. It does not authorize copying protected expression, redistribution, pattern admission or promotion to NOXIA authority.

## Future acquisition gate

A remote-only source may gain a local copy only after recording an explicit licence/reuse basis, an original official URL, retrieval date, unchanged file digest, version/currentness and any source-specific attribution conditions. Restricted material must never be published through this repository.
